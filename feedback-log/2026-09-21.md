### 2. New: the overdue headline undercounts the backlog by 38%, and hides every phrase


The bucket table two fields over comes from `getOverdueQueue()` (`lib/db/pedagogy-queries.ts:346-370`), which
unions `user_words` **and `user_phrases`**, filters `status <> 'new'`, and counts everything already due.

| | headline `overdueReviews` | `pedagogy.overdue` buckets |
|---|---|---|
| Phrases | **excluded entirely** | included (105 late, 81 of them 8d+) |
| Freshly due | excluded (needs >2d late) | included ("due now", "1–2d late") |
| `status = 'new'` | not filtered | filtered out |

Today that means the headline reads **190** while the queue actually holds **305 late items** — 200 words +
105 phrases — of which **264 are more than 8 days late**. The 190 is arithmetically correct for what it asks
(183 words at 8d+ plus 7 at 3–7d); it simply asks a narrower question than its name implies, and the name is
what gets read at 03:30.

I checked this because the two numbers looked contradictory and I expected to find a bug. **There is no bug** —
both queries do exactly what they say. The defect is in the label, and it matters only because this is the one
retention number anyone looks at. Recording it so the next reader doesn't re-derive it, and so nobody "fixes"
the buckets to match the headline, which would be the wrong direction.
