### 2. Re-verified from source: the phrase-side telemetry double-count is real

Yesterday's finding was an inference from the equal counts. I read the components today and it holds, with
exact sites:

- `PhraseDrillBlock` fires `phrase_drill_correct` / `phrase_drill_wrong` carrying `payload.cueType`
  (`components/learn/PhraseDrillBlock.tsx:137,159`).
- It renders the *word-level* `Cloze` (`:207`) and `ProductionTyping` (`:239`) as its cue children, passing
  only `onCorrect` / `onAnswer` — **there is no surface or cueType prop**, so nothing can suppress the child's
  own telemetry.
- Those children fire unconditionally: `production_correct` / `production_close_correct` / `production_wrong`
  (`ProductionTyping.tsx:106,158,195`) and `cloze_correct` / `cloze_wrong` (`Cloze.tsx:102,149,180`).
- `CUE_FROM_EVENT` (`lib/db/pedagogy-queries.ts:28-35`) files the parent event under `phrase_production` /
  `phrase_cloze` and the child event under `production` / `cloze`.

(34 == 34, 21 == 21). The `production` and `cloze` rows on `/admin/pedagogy` are not word-level measurements;
the production child is even handed `wordId={phrase.id}` (`PhraseDrillBlock.tsx:246`), so its payload id is a
phrase id. This is the failure mode `CLAUDE.md` names under Measurement: a row that reads as a real measurement
and isn't one.

`phrase_production`. Word-level `DrillBlock` emitted nothing in 30 days. (Note this is sound *because* of the
